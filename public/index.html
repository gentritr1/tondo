        How to play

        • Match the top card by topping (colour) or by number — or play a Wild and pick the next topping.
        • Nothing to play? Draw one card. If it fits you may play it at once; otherwise keep it and pass.
        • ⊘ Skip makes the next player lose their turn. +2 makes them draw two and lose their turn. ⇄ Flip reverses the play order (with two players it acts as a Skip).
        • Down to two cards? Call TONDO before playing one of them. Forget, and anyone who catches you makes you draw two.
        • First to zero wins the slice and scores the cards left in every other hand: numbers at face value, action cards 20 points, a Wild 50.
        • Four slices make a pie — highest score takes it.

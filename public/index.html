    How to play

    • Match the top card by topping (colour) or by number — or play a Wild and pick the next topping.
    • Nothing to play? Draw one card. If it fits you may play it at once; otherwise keep it and pass.
    • ⊘ Skip makes the next player lose their turn. +2 makes them draw two and lose their turn. ↻ Reverse flips the play order.
    • Down to two cards? Call TONDO before playing one of them. Forget, and anyone who catches you makes you draw two.
    • First to zero cards wins the round.
